Our Story

How SmileCare Began

SmileCare Dental was founded in 2012 by Dr. Ahmed Raza with a single treatment chair and one clear belief — that every person deserves honest, high-quality dental care in a comfortable setting.

What started as a small clinic in Gulberg has grown into a full-service dental studio with 15 specialists, 8 modern treatment rooms, and over 8,500 patients who trust us with their smiles.

Our growth has never changed our core commitment — treating every patient like family and every smile like it matters, because it does.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional dental care that improves the quality of life of our patients — through clinical excellence, compassion, and a commitment to making every visit as comfortable as possible.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted dental clinic in Pakistan — where patients feel safe, informed, and genuinely cared for. We want healthy smiles to be accessible to every family in our community.
